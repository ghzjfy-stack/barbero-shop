## Rank — scroll-stop curiosity (men 22–40)

1. **A — למה הקופסה הזו לא ירוקה?**  
   Strongest pattern interrupt. Green = Rogaine is culturally coded for this audience; a white can + “not green?” creates instant visual dissonance before any sell.

2. **B — רגע… זה לא Rogaine?**  
   Fast recognition stop, but naming Rogaine early can siphon attention toward the green brand and feels one beat closer to a “generic vs brand” ad.

3. **C — למה בארה״ב לוקחים לבן?**  
   Solid social-proof curiosity, weaker without a clear green-vs-white visual mismatch in the first frame.

### Winner: **A**
