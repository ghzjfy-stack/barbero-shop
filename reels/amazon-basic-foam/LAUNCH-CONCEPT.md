# Amazon Basic Care — launch reel concept

MinoxShop · `amazon-basic-foam-man` · men 5% foam · 3×2.11oz · **₪395** · USA import · free shipping  
15s · 1080×1920 · IG / TikTok · Hebrew chips only, upper third · soft room tone · CTA **הקישור בפרופיל**

Pack fact used for the hook: the men’s carton is **blue / navy**, the can is **blue**. Men’s Rogaine foam is a blue carton and a **gray** can. A “why isn’t it green?” hook is false for both. The carton also prints “Compare to Men’s Rogaine® active ingredient” — that line stays out of frame.

## Three hooks

| | Hook | Words | Stop | Curiosity | Honesty | Why it places |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **A** | מה יש בקופסה הכחולה מארה״ב? | 5 | **8** | **8** | **8** | Object + question. Brand, price, and the link stay unresolved. Color matches the real carton. |
| B | השם על הקופסה לא מה שחשבתם | 6 | 7 | 8 | 7 | Strong withhold, but the gotcha points at “you thought it was the famous one” and slides toward a comparison ad. |
| C | 395 שקל על שלושה חודשי קצף | 6 | 6 | 4 | 9 | True, and it is the catalog frame. Nothing left to tap for except a link with no loop. |

**Winner: A.** C is the honest price tag and the scroll-past. B is curious and riskier: a blue minoxidil carton next to “not what you thought” reads as a Rogaine substitute bit without saying the sentence. A asks what the object is, then answers with the product facts, and leaves only the profile as the way to get it.

## Beat sheet — 15s, winner A

One handheld counter. No white seamless, no price card, no grid, no logo sting. Chips: Hebrew, large, RTL, upper third (about 18–28% from the top). No lower-third text. Audio: quiet room, the cap and the foam puff at low level, no trend track, no voiceover, no hit on the first frame.

## One risk

The front of the carton prints “Compare to Men’s Rogaine® active ingredient.” A wide shot puts a forbidden equality claim on screen, and comments will complete it — Rogaine men’s foam on this shop is ₪400, so a secret-bargain read dies in one reply. Keep that English line out of every frame, and do not answer it with a Hebrew chip. The price chip stays **395₪** with no “במקום”.
